Umrah Guide: Airport to Completion

Four pillars of Umrah: Ihram, Tawaf, Sa'i, and Halq/Taqsir.

1. Before Travel

  • Valid passport and Umrah visa
  • Make sincere tawbah and settle debts
  • Men: two white unstitched cloths. Women: modest clothes
  • Use unscented soap only

2. Airport and Plane

  1. Do ghusl and wear Ihram before the Miqat (often on the plane)
  2. Pray 2 rakat if the time allows
  3. Make the intention when the pilot announces the Miqat

4. Rules of Ihram

  • No cutting hair or nails, no perfume
  • No marital relations, no arguing or sinning
  • Men: no stitched clothes, no head covering
  • Women: no face veil (niqab) or gloves

  2. Keep the Kaaba on your left
  3. Men: brisk walk in first 3 rounds and right shoulder uncovered

  • Women: cut a fingertip length from the hair ends
